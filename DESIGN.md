# Swyft design direction

Swyft helps customers reserve a service time and lets owners run an orderly day.

- Character: brisk, composed, a little warm.
- World: a well-kept appointment book, with a clear daily schedule and precise time marks.
- Palette: warm paper, dark ink, and a single deep teal accent for actions and availability.
- Moments: the day and time picker, and a confirmation ticket showing the exact booking.
- Signature: a fine timeline rule beside appointment details.
- Motion: fast press feedback and a restrained confirmation entrance; routine navigation stays immediate.

The mobile and admin surfaces are operational tools. Legibility, safe areas, clear status, and predictable controls take priority over decoration.
